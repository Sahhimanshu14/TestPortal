UPSC Combined Defence Services Examination — Official Instructions

1. General Instructions

  • The candidate must adhere to the 2-hour duration for Paper I, II, and III.
  • Each question has four alternative responses. Only one response should be selected.
  • Candidates are strictly assessed according to UPSC Combined Defence Services examination norms.

2. Marking Scheme & Penalties

  • Paper I (English) & Paper II (GS): +0.833 Marks per correct answer | -0.277 Marks penalty for every incorrect response (1/3rd deduction).
  • Paper III (Mathematics): +1.0 Mark per correct answer | -0.333 Marks penalty for every incorrect response.
  • No deduction is made for unattempted questions.
  • Multiple choices selected for a single item are marked incorrect.

3. Sectional Qualifying Benchmark

  • Candidates must secure a mandatory minimum of 20% in each individual paper to be considered for merit list admission to IMA, INA, AFA, and OTA.
